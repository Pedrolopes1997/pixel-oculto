Pixel Oculto Uploader

An internal publishing tool for two YouTube channels about gaming and tech history: @OPixelOculto (Portuguese) and @HiddenPixelHQ (English).

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.
  • Fills in the title, description, tags, category and language of each video, and schedules it.
  • Reads only the basic info of the connected channel, to confirm the upload goes to the right channel.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the two channels. It is not offered to the public and there are no other users.
